## 1. Introduction

Myelin Oligodendrocyte Glycoprotein Antibody-Associated Disease (MOGAD) is an autoimmune inflammatory demyelinating disorder of the central nervous system (CNS) that has been established as a distinct entity from multiple sclerosis (MS) and aquaporin-4-IgG neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorders (AQP4-NMOSD). MOGAD is characterized by immune-mediated demyelination primarily affecting the optic nerves, brain, and spinal cord.

### Key Distinguishing Features of Pediatric MOGAD
- Age-dependent clinical phenotypes
- Generally favorable long-term prognosis compared to AQP4-NMOSD
- High response rates to immunotherapy
- Variable disease course (monophasic vs. relapsing)
- Unique neuroimaging patterns

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
